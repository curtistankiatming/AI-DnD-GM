# Job balance pass, 11 November 2026

Step 6 of the job advancement plan. This report records simulated battles; it
is not natural play and not browser play.

## What was simulated

- **Source:** branch `claude/project-thread-34kxxs` (after) against `master` at
  `cac2f9a` (before), both run with the same `scripts/balance-sim.js`.
- **Runtime:** Node v22.23.4, Linux, seeded dice, no AI or network calls.
- **Battle:** the standard AI-adventure battle (a boss and two retainers), built
  by the engine from the director test fixture. The enemy theme rotates by seed,
  so one battle in six has an undead boss that resists weapon damage.
- **Party:** the hero with the jobs due at that level (2nd job from 20, 3rd from
  40, 4th from 70) and the two starting companions, all at full health.
- **Matrix:** 6 classes × 3 branches × 9 levels (20 to 100 by 10) × 3
  difficulties × 8 seeds = 3,888 battles per run with job skills and as many
  without.
- **Hero policy:** focus the weakest enemy, use job skills while Advancement
  lasts (ultimate first, then in the order learnt; healing and protection only
  when someone is below 60%), Hunter's Mark for rangers, then the class's plain
  attack. A second run uses the most recently learnt skill first
  (`--policy newest`). Companions and enemies use the game's own AI.

Run it with `npm run balance` (about seven minutes); `--seeds`, `--levels`,
`--tiers`, `--classes`, `--policy` and `--json` narrow or record a run.

## What changed

- **Fighters strike three times** with each Attack from level 20 (twice from 5,
  as before). Before this, every fighter branch lost most Heroic battles.
- **Weapon skills never make fewer strikes than the Attack action.** A one-strike
  skill gets plain follow-up strikes, so a fighter is not punished for using it.
- **Objective skills:** apart from ultimates, no skill moves a battle objective
  more than two steps (Hidden Route, Terms of Surrender and Royal Decree now
  move one; Collapse two; Worldwalk no longer moves it).
- **Weaker branches:** more damage for Chronist (Stasis, Time Stop, two damage
  passives), Binder, Lorekeeper (Recital of Ages, Vast Learning), Saboteur
  (Tripwire Net, Controlled Blast), Evoker (Thunderclap) and Warlord passives.
- **Battle Priest:** Templar Charge loses its extra damage; Hallowed Onslaught
  strikes twice instead of three times.
- **Late-game enemy health:** AI-adventure enemies gain extra health from level
  70, rising to +25% at 100 (`XP.vigor`). The XP rating uses the same value, so
  the standard battle still rates Hard.

## Reading the results

- The fighter fix is the main change: all three branches went from losing most
  Heroic battles to winning three in four or better.
- The Chronist moved from the weakest branch to a mid-table one.
- Levels 20 to 70 got easier on Hard and Heroic; the extra health brings 80 to
  100 back toward the middle. A hero who has just picked a 2nd job at 20 is
  still the most likely to lose a Heroic battle (58%).
- Casters depend heavily on play style. Evoker, Exorcist, Lorekeeper and
  Saboteur lose more when they spend Advancement on their earliest skills, and
  win 86 to 99% of Heroic battles when they use their newest skills first.
- Pathfinder wins by moving the objective. Played newest-skill-first it uses
  its control skills instead and loses most battles. This is the policy, not
  a player who goes for the objective, but it shows the branch has little
  direct damage.
- Battle Priest and Lifewarden remain the strongest; their strength comes
  mostly from the cleric class (Guiding Bolt and healing), not the job skills.

## Limitations

- Eight seeds per cell: a single cell can move by 10 to 15 points between runs.
  Compare branch and level averages, not single cells.
- The policies are simple. Real players use items, rests, orders, the
  objective and the peaceful resolution, none of which the policy explores.
- Only the AI-adventure battle is simulated; the authored levels 1 to 10 and
  job trials are not.
- Renown boons after level 100 are not simulated.
