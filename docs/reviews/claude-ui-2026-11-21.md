# Visual UI review, 21 November 2026

Scope: visual UI only (layout, consistency, readability) on phone (390 × 844),
320 px and laptop (1366 × 800). A separate review covers UX flows. Saves used: a
level 5 fight, a level 12 bounty fight, level 13 in Merrowgate, and a level 13
story check that Maren rolls.

Curtis was away, so the open design questions went to two independent reviewer
agents. Each looked at the game on its own and recommended an answer; this PR
adopts what both agreed on.

## Decisions on the three open items

| Question | Reviewer A | Reviewer B | Adopted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Act / Say / Ask buttons | Shrink and merge into a small switch by the input | Shrink into a small 3-way switch at the left of the input | A small segmented switch on the same row as the input, on every screen and in fights. |
| "Prepare" tab name | Rename to **Town** | Rename to **Town** | **Town** (the internal id stays `prepare`). |
| Phone combat board 2–3 screens tall | Hide the message box in fights, compact action cards, shorter log, compact orders and fighter cards | The same, in nearly the same order | All of these. See below. |

## What changed

**Combat board**
- In a fight, on every screen: the AI tip, the campaign-instructions link and the How to play link
  leave the message box. The AI status line (thinking, not applied, routing notices) stays.
- Phones: the message box is one row in a fight: the small Act/Say/Ask switch, the box and Send.
  The switch stays because Say and Ask are the typed modes that work during a fight. The scene header is
  hidden because the board has its own title. The turn-order strip is smaller, the log is capped
  at about four lines, and fighter cards are tighter. Action cards show one line of what they do.
  An unavailable action shows why instead. The readied action's full description appears
  under "Your turn", since phones have no tooltips.
- Fighter names wrap instead of being cut off ("Sister Maren …", "Wrecker Ca…"). Cards share
  the row's width.
- Buffs and debuffs are separate chips. They are green when the effect helps your side and red when it hurts it,
  so a buff on an enemy is red. Each chip also shows the rounds left ("Guarded · 1 rd"). "Tactical encouragement from Sister
  Maren Vale" now reads "Tactical encouragement".
- "Combine: Sister" is now "Combine: Maren".
- Laptop: the board is centred in the window during a fight.
- At 320 px the enemy and party cards stay in two columns.

**Items**
- Rarity is a coloured label: Common (plain), Uncommon green, Rare blue, Epic purple and Legendary
  amber. It appears on pack cards, worn gear and shop rows.
- "Requires: Level 6 · Dexterity 13" has its own line. It turns red, with "not yet for you", when the
  hero doesn't meet it ("not for your class" when it is a class limit). When nobody in the party can wear the item, the Equip button reads
  **Locked** and is disabled.

**Town (was Prepare)**
- The bounty board is its own card under the expedition board, with an amber edge. It has XP and gold
  chips, the loss cost, and a **Take a bounty** button. Before, it was a lodging tile.
- Service tile titles are now larger than their descriptions. They used to be smaller.

**Story screen**
- A story check names who rolled first and in bold: "**Maren rolls** · Insight 18 + 8 = 26 against 17".
- The conversation strip no longer repeats lines that a button already produced, such as story choices
  and combat actions ("Wren attacks Wrecker Captain" × 8 after a fight). A rules line still shows
  when it follows the player's typed message or the game master's reply.
- Party strip on phones uses first names (Orin, Maren, Nessa) instead of "Orin Ston…".
- With the AI game master off, phones no longer show the "Want to try your own ideas?" row under
  every turn. The same tip is in How to play and in Settings. "How to play" is hidden during fights.
- "Back to your message" on the other tabs now reads "Back to adventure".

## Left for later (single-reviewer or larger ideas)

- Collapsible item cards on the Party pack (B), and item skills as their own chip (A and B).
- A sticky End Turn bar on phones (A).
- The laptop expedition card uses half the width. The side quest could sit beside it (B).
- The "Saved-game recap" paragraph in the combat log (B).
- The toast covering the top bar for a few seconds on phones (B).
- The page's own scroll height on phones is taller than the story box (it was before this change too).
