# Choices that still led nowhere: third pass (22 November 2026)

Curtis asked that player choices matter and change the story. The first two
passes covered Nessa's answer about Wren's post, Hollin's Reach, six camp
choices and Orin's and Maren's endings. This pass covers what was left.

## Side jobs whose endings were never told again

Each of these side jobs already recorded how it ended. Nothing read that
record, so the ending was forgotten as soon as the job closed. Each ending
now has its own line in the next epilogue.

| Side job | Where it is remembered | Endings told |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pim's Night Bell | Book Two epilogue, Dera's forge | the rota, Saturdays only, you rang the hour, you told Tovin |
| The Midwinter Well | Book Two epilogue | shared by the bucket, Aveline's ruling, dug together, you paid |
| The Driver's Wages | Book Two epilogue | Iona hired Willem, the council paid in spring, you opened the box (witnessed if Orin was there, "count unknown" if not) |
| The Name on the Boat | Book Two epilogue | the LENORA still sails out of Merrowgate |
| The apprentice and the roster | Book Three epilogue | four endings each |
| The winter beds at Highpass | Book Three epilogue | Iona and Fennick together, the shared bunks |
| The lamp oil at Saltwick | Book Three epilogue | Joss drives it himself now |
| The Carr brothers | Book Three epilogue | runners or volunteers, unless Vell was emptied |

The beds, oil and brothers jobs had no record of how they ended, so they now
set one.

## Camp choices that only moved approval

- **The wall at Highpass ("Send it, Nessa").** Nessa keeps the goat letter.
  In Book Two's epilogue she puts it in the same envelope as her answer to Wren.
- **Dera's forge ("not tonight" or "what happened at Saltwick?").** When you
  sit with Orin after the tide, he answers the way you asked: he thanks you
  for waiting, or he apologises for walking out.
- **The road out of Vell ("keep one name").** At the seal, Maren tells you
  Ysolde's name is still inside the tin lid, with nothing written beside it.
- **The last evening at home ("Send it this time").** In the company chapter,
  Ysolde has Nessa's goat letter in her apron pocket. This line plays only if
  Nessa is with you.

## One behaviour change

A camp scene that records a choice can't be left halfway, like every other
camp scene that records one. The home-camp scene before Vell now records a
choice, so leaving to town from it is no longer allowed. A test that used it
as the example of a camp you can leave now uses the pass camp.

## Left as they are

The bridge, vote, harbour, rite, pass, Vell and Hollin camp scenes are
conversations. Their choices change only how a companion feels about you,
which is what they are for.
