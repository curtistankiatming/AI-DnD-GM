# Player experience review, 21 November 2026

An independent review of flows and clarity, not of the visual look (the UI
review covers that). It covers the first-time experience, whether the new
systems explain themselves, the feedback after each action, and the
end-of-book and paid-tier messages.

**Method.** I played the opening in the browser with the listed choices (no
AI key). Then I played whole campaigns with the test player
(`tests/player-policy.js`) as a fighter, a bard and a cleric. I saved the game
at the first fight, in town at levels 3 and 12, after a companion walked out
(forced by setting that chapter's approval count), and after the last written
chapter, and loaded each save in the browser on phone (390 px) and laptop
(1280 px) to read the screens. I read every event line the game printed in
those runs (about 15,000). For the paid-tier messages I used
`Book.grant(state, 2)` with `BRIARWATCH_PREVIEW_BOOKS=1`, which is what a
free account on the hosted game sees once Book Three is out.

Screenshots: `/mnt/project-files/ux-review/`.

## What works

- **The first screen** teaches by showing. The opening scene is good, the
  "How to play" card is three short lines, and each choice says its chance
  and who rolls. "Sister Maren Vale rolls Investigation +0 against 11 · you
  would have 45%" explains the companion roll better than any help text.
- **Companion rolls** are clear before and after: the choice card names the
  roller, the result line says "Sister Maren Vale is best placed for this and
  takes it on", and the "Best person rolls / I roll every check" switch is
  right above the choices.
- **Falling behind** is explained where you decide: the chapter row on the
  story screen says how many levels under you are and, past 5, exactly what
  changes (+N to hit, N×10% damage, checks N harder), and points to bounties.
- **The bounty board** row says what you fight, where, what it pays, what
  losing costs, and that it repeats. It says when it pays extra while you are
  behind.
- **The walk-out scenes** themselves are good. Orin says why in his own
  words, the game says when he will be back, and the Party tab shows him as
  "Away · Back once The Harbour Bell is done".
- **Gear requirements** are written plainly ("Requires: Rogue, Ranger or
  Bard", "Needs Strength 13 (you have 8)"), and the equip dialog greys out
  anyone who can't wear it.

## Findings

Ordered by how much they would hurt a real player. "Fixed" means fixed in
this PR.

### 1. A free hosted player is steered into losing Book Three (fixed)

At the end of Book Two on a free account, the message said, in order: the
written story is complete; ask the game master for a new adventure; "without
it, your written campaign ends here"; and only then, "The story goes on in
Book 3 … for paid accounts; this save opens it straight away on an upgrade,
**as long as it has not begun an AI adventure of its own**."

So it first told the player to start an AI adventure, then said that doing so
closes Book Three to the save for good (`Book.aiStory`, `Book.finished`). A
player who follows the first instruction loses the second. The "Ask for a new
adventure" card on the story screen did not mention it at all.

Fix: the message now leads with the paid book and states the trade-off once,
plainly: "The story goes on in Book 3, The Names in the Bronze, for paid
accounts: upgrade, and this save carries straight on into it. If you would
rather keep going now, the AI storyteller can write adventures … But once this
save starts one, it follows its own story and will not go back to Book 3." The
wish card says the same before you ask, and Prepare says so too. Once a save
has gone its own way, Prepare says that instead of offering the upgrade.

Whether an AI adventure *should* close the paid book is a business rule for
Curtis (see "Questions for Curtis"); this PR only makes the current rule
visible.

### 2. Walk-outs come without warning (fixed)

A companion walks out if their approval falls by 2 or more over one chapter in
Book Two. Nothing said so until the morning they left. The only signs were
"Orin Stoneward disapproved (-1 bond)" lines, which look like every other
bond change, and the Party tab could still show him as "Devoted +5", because
the walk-out counts this chapter's change, not the bond.

Fix: when a companion's count for the chapter reaches the line, the story
says so right after the bond line: "Orin Stoneward is unhappy with how this
chapter is going. If it ends like this, he may leave the party for a while.
Choices he agrees with can still change his mind." If later choices bring him
back over the line: "Orin Stoneward seems easier about how this chapter is
going." While he is at the line, his Party card says "Unhappy with this
chapter's choices. May leave the party for a while if it ends like this."
Book One, and the camp after Book One's last chapter, never warn, because
they never end in a walk-out.

### 3. After the last chapter, the story screen forgets the story ended (fixed)

"The written story is complete …" is printed once, as an event. Reload the
save and the story screen shows only a bounty board and a greyed "Ask for a
new adventure". A returning player has no idea the books are over.

Fix: "What's next?" now always says it: "The written story is complete. New
adventures come from the AI game master; the bounty board stays open." (or
the paid-book line on a free hosted account).

### 4. The shop lets you buy gear nobody can wear (fixed)

Shop cards print "Requires: Rogue, Ranger or Bard", but whether anyone in
your party qualifies was hidden inside the collapsed "Compare with party
equipment". A fighter could buy an 82-gold Veilstep Cloak and find out at the
equip dialog.

Fix: a shop card for an item with requirements now says who can use it: "Can
be used by Nessa Reed.", "Everyone in your party can use this." or, in
warning colour, "No one in your party can use this yet."

### 5. Side jobs are only on Prepare (fixed)

Book Two's side jobs are a real part of the book (and the falling-behind note
recommends them), but they were listed only under Prepare → Expeditions. The
story screen's "What's next?" showed the chapter, the bounty board and the AI
card.

Fix: "What's next?" now lists up to two side jobs you can take in this town,
with "Take the job". Jobs in other towns stay on Prepare.

### 6. Designer wording in player messages (fixed)

Several lines read like notes to the developer. Rewritten:

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| Town stock refreshed after new progress, not after opening a menu. | The shops have new stock. |
| The costly outcome reduced payment, not experience. | The pay is lower for how costly it was; the XP is not. |
| Civic lodging restores HP, hit dice and class resources without cost. Poison requires treatment; existing protective coatings remain. No XP or stock refresh is granted. | A free night at the town lodging restores HP, hit dice and class abilities. Poison still needs the shrine. |
| Once per progress cycle. / …already been claimed this progress cycle. Free recovery remains available. | Once after each expedition. / You have already had the inn's paid stay since your last expedition. The free lodging is still open. |
| Free recovery prevents an empty purse from ending the adventure. Shops restock only after a new expedition is resolved. | The town lodging is always free, so an empty purse never ends the adventure. Shops get new stock each time you finish an expedition. |
| This is the maximum forge tier in this campaign. | The smith cannot improve it further. |
| (Shuttered Lantern) Routine fuel tracking is deliberately omitted. | (removed) |
| Companions provide covering help for the hero's next objective check instead of attacking when help is still needed. | Companions help with the objective instead of attacking, giving your next objective check advantage. |
| Rowan works toward release the wagon drivers: 1/2. | Rowan works to release the wagon drivers: 1/2. (Proper names such as Iona keep their capital.) |

### 7. Scene text says "you" when a companion rolled (not fixed)

Companions now make about 59% of written checks, but the outcome text was
written for the hero: "Under the rain-dark fibres **you** find a thumbprint of
black wax" right after "Sister Maren Vale rolled 13". The line before it names
the roller, so it reads as the party's "you" and is not confusing, only a
little off. Fixing it properly means rewriting outcome text check by check;
that belongs with the story thread. A cheap middle step would be to give the
most-taken checks a companion version.

### 8. Smaller things (not fixed)

- **Short rest at full health.** On the very first screen, the first button
  under "What do you do?" is "Take short rest", with the whole party at full
  HP. It uses up that scene's rest for nothing. Hide it when nobody is hurt
  and no rest-based ability is spent.
- **Orders have no explanation.** "Focus fire / Protect ally / Cover
  objective" show no text until you use one. A one-line tooltip each would do.
- **Bounty difficulty.** The board says "a fight at your level", and the
  result says "Bounty claimed (hard battle)". Say "a hard fight" up front.
- **Three AI prompts on the first screen.** How to play, "Ask for a side
  quest: Locked", and "Turn on the AI game master" under the box all point to
  the AI before the player has made a choice. One is enough there.
- **The ending message is long.** Five sentences at the end of Book Two; it is
  the right content, but it could be two.
- **Book numbering.** Messages say "Book 2", the story bible and some cards
  say "Book Two". Pick one for players.
