# Stage 2: a connected chat-led adventure

Optional **Lantern Road: A Promise in the Rain** starts in Briarwatch via the
chat button or `/journey`. It has three actual locations: broken wagon, flooded
crossing and infirmary. `/pause` returns home without resetting checks, delays,
relationships or promises. The existing main campaign and courier remain intact.

Visible approaches and a small conservative offline grammar support the actual
repair-instead-of-payment action, investigation, sealed-letter promises, owned
rope, negotiation, a free slower alternative, medical aid and delivery. Every
attempt is previewed and requires confirmation. An enabled local model may map
other phrasing to an offered action, but cannot commit invented rules or facts.
This is a bounded authored adventure, not unrestricted world generation.

Preparation and earlier decisions affect later mechanics: investigation lowers
repair difficulty; Orin and Maren can provide relevant help while conscious;
Tamsin's trust changes the ferry difficulty; a verified route improves rope use.
The bandit claim is explicitly a rumor until evidence proves this axle failed
through wood-rot. A promised letter remains pending until delivered unopened,
or becomes broken if the player explicitly opens it. Slower safe choices are
always available without money, so a failed roll does not halt the adventure.

All resolutions receive the same 120 objective XP once. Timely delivery earns
25 gold; delay of 3 or more earns 15. Positive trust adds one trade reputation
and one collectable herbs bundle back in town. Pausing, repeated requests,
save/load and menu reopening do not repeat a reward or reset an attempted check.

Stage 2 tests distinguish diagnostic forced-roll fixtures from normal browser
interaction. Live model interpretation and prose quality remain untested.
Stage 3 adds the journal; this stage already saves authoritative journey facts.
